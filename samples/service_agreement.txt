Service Agreement

This agreement is made on March 3, 2026 between Nile Logistics LLC, based in Cairo, Egypt, and Brightline Software Ltd, based in London, United Kingdom.

Brightline Software will build and deliver an order-tracking dashboard for Nile Logistics. The project starts on March 10, 2026 and the first working version must be delivered by April 15, 2026.

The total fee is USD 4,800. Nile Logistics will pay 50% (USD 2,400) when the agreement is signed and the remaining USD 2,400 after final delivery. Hosting costs of USD 60 per month are paid separately by Nile Logistics.

Sarah Ahmed is the project manager for Nile Logistics. James Carter leads the work for Brightline Software. Both sides must confirm the final feature list by March 20, 2026.

The team was very happy with the first workshop. However, the client is worried that the delivery date is too tight.
